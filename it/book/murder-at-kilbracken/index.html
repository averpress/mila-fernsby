Leggila in inglese

What if the murder you have to solve is the one you wrote, and threw away?

Ailsa Kerr used to be head writer on Britain's most-watched crime drama. Now she scripts murder-mystery weekends for a company two months from bankruptcy, and this one cannot be cancelled. A houseful of guests has paid about £850 each to spend it at Kilbracken Castle, on a tidal island off the Scottish coast. Her Inspector is Desmond Hale, the actor who played Harlow for six years and the only witness in her lawsuit over who created the show. Their lawyers have forbidden them to speak to each other. Neither of them has managed to keep to it.

On Friday night the body in the library is meant to be a joke. It isn't. The killing follows a version of the script that Ailsa rejected, the tide has shut the causeway until Sunday, the phone lines are down, and every guest has signed a waiver for "simulated emergencies" and believes nothing they are told. If Ailsa stops the game, Foxglove Events goes under. If she keeps it running, the killer is sitting at her dinner table, and the one man who can help her find them is the man she is not allowed to trust.

The tide turns on Sunday morning. Will the killer still be at the table when it does?
